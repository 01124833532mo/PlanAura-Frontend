How Planura Works

Planura connects you with vetted event vendors and keeps discovery, comparison, and booking in one place — so getting from idea to confirmed event takes minutes, not weeks of back and forth.

01 search

Discover

Browse services by category, or explore vendors directly with filters for city, price, rating, and availability.

02 compare_arrows

Compare

Every vendor profile shows real portfolios, published packages, and genuine client reviews, so you can compare options with confidence.

03 credit_card

Book

Pick a date, review your booking agreement, and pay securely through Stripe. Your card is only charged once the vendor confirms.

04 celebration

Enjoy

Track every booking in one place, message and coordinate with your vendors, and enjoy an event brought to life by the right team.

Why Planura

Planning an event usually means juggling phone calls, scattered quotes, and vendors you've never worked with before. Planura brings that into a single, trustworthy platform: transparent pricing on every package, verified vendor profiles, and a booking flow that keeps your payment protected until the vendor actually confirms your date.

Whether you're planning a wedding, a birthday, or a corporate event, you browse and book on your own terms — no account required until you're ready to reserve a date.
