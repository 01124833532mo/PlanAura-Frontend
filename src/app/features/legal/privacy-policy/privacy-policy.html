Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Planura collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our platform to plan events, browse Vendors, and manage bookings. By using Planura, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

assignment

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your full name, email address, phone number, password (stored securely as a hash), and account role (Client, Vendor, or Admin).
  • Profile information — your profile picture, city, and date of birth (optional); for Vendors, your business name, description, category, address, logo, cover image, and portfolio media.
  • Verification documents — for Vendors, identification and business documents submitted as part of our vendor verification process.
  • Event & booking information — event plans (title, event type, date, city, guest count, budget, style notes), booking requests, and related communications.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment partner, Stripe; we receive confirmation and transaction details but do not store your full card number.
  • Usage information — log data, device and browser information, and how you interact with the Platform, collected automatically.
settings

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your access to the Platform.
  • To connect Clients with Vendors, process bookings, and facilitate payments.
  • To verify Vendor identity and business information as part of our vendor approval process.
  • To communicate with you about your account, bookings, and Platform updates.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To improve and personalize the Platform, including troubleshooting and analytics.
lock

3. Account & Authentication Data

We use secure authentication practices — including hashed passwords and token-based sessions — to protect your account. We never store your password in plain text. Your account role (Client, Vendor, or Admin) determines which features and data you can access on the Platform.

event_available

4. Booking & Vendor Information

When you create an event plan or submit a booking request, we collect the details necessary to connect you with the right Vendor and process the booking, such as event date, location, guest count, and selected package. Vendor profile information — business name, category, packages, pricing, availability, portfolio, and reviews — is shown to Clients to help them make informed booking decisions.

Booking history and payment status are retained to support customer service, dispute resolution, and legal or accounting requirements.

cookie

5. Cookies & Analytics

Planura uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Platform is used. We may use analytics tools to measure traffic and engagement in aggregate, which helps us improve the Platform.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings; note that disabling cookies may affect certain features, such as staying signed in.

security

6. Data Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure infrastructure — to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor; Planura does not store your full payment card details on its own servers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to continuously review and improve our safeguards.

7. Data Sharing

  • With Vendors and Clients as necessary to facilitate bookings — for example, a Vendor receives the event details relevant to a booking request.
  • With trusted service providers who help us operate the Platform, such as our payment processor (Stripe) and hosting/infrastructure providers, under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Planura, our users, or the public.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

8. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update most of your account and profile information directly from your profile page. For account deletion or other data-related requests, contact us using the details below.

We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Vendor verification documents and booking/payment records may be retained longer where required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Planura is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the Platform or by email.
